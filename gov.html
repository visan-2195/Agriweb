🌾 Government Schemes for Farmers

PM-KISAN (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi)

Financial assistance of ₹6,000/year to small and marginal farmers, directly credited to their bank accounts in 3 equal installments.

  • Launched: 2019
  • Eligibility: Land-owning farmers
  • Apply: Online or via agriculture officer
Official Website
PMFBY (Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana)

Crop insurance scheme protecting against droughts, pests, and natural disasters. Low premium and wide coverage.

  • Kharif: 2% premium | Rabi: 1.5%
  • Eligibility: All farmers with insurable crops
  • Claim through local bank/insurance office
Official Website
Soil Health Card Scheme

Provides nutrient status of soil with advice on fertilizers to improve productivity and avoid excessive chemical use.

  • Issued every 2 years
  • Lab-tested soil sample report
  • Free service under GOI
Visit Portal
Kisan Credit Card (KCC)

Offers short-term credit at low interest for seeds, fertilizers, and other farm needs.

  • Interest: As low as 4% (with subsidy)
  • Loan limit: Based on land and crop type
  • Apply at your bank with land documents
KCC Details
e-NAM (National Agriculture Market)

Online trading platform to help farmers get better market prices by connecting them to buyers across India.

  • More than 1000 mandis connected
  • Real-time price discovery
  • Transparent transactions
Go to eNAM
RKVY (Rashtriya Krishi Vikas Yojana)

Supports infrastructure, technology, and value chain development for state-level agriculture.

  • State-driven planning
  • Focus on modernization
  • Funding based on state proposals
